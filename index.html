    🍯 Homemade Mixers

    Make these kitchen staples yourself, then check them off in My Ingredients. Use clean containers and refrigerate promptly.

    Simple syrup (1:1)

    Combine 1 cup white sugar and 1 cup water. Warm gently, stirring until dissolved. Do not boil unnecessarily. Cool, bottle, label and refrigerate. Makes approximately 1½ cups. Discard if cloudy, moldy or off-smelling; use within about 2 weeks when handled cleanly.

    Rich simple syrup (2:1)

    Combine 2 cups sugar and 1 cup water. Heat gently while stirring until fully dissolved. Cool and refrigerate. Sweeter and thicker; use less than standard simple syrup to taste.

    Honey syrup

    Mix 1 part honey with 1 part warm water until smooth. Cool and refrigerate. Use in cocktails that call for honey syrup.

    Ginger syrup

    Simmer ½ cup sliced fresh ginger with 1 cup water for 10 minutes. Strain, stir in 1 cup sugar until dissolved, cool and refrigerate. Use promptly, ideally within 1 week.

    For egg-white cocktails, consider pasteurized egg whites. Avoid serving raw eggs to people at higher risk of foodborne illness.
